# Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project is an Insurance Risk & Claim Analysis dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes insurance policies, claim amounts, customer demographics, vehicle information, and risk-related factors to provide meaningful business insights.

The project demonstrates skills in data analysis, data visualization, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design.

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze insurance policy and claim data and identify patterns related to:

- Insurance policies
- Claim amounts
- Claim frequency
- Vehicle characteristics
- Customer demographics
- Coverage zones
- Age groups
- Education
- Marital status
- Car usage
- Car make and year

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Total Policies by Car Use

The dashboard compares insurance policies based on car usage, including:

- Private
- Commercial

Private vehicle usage represents the larger share of policies in the dashboard.

### 2. Total Policies by Car Make

The dashboard analyzes policy distribution across different vehicle manufacturers.

Major car makes shown include:

- Ford
- Chevrolet
- Dodge
- Toyota
- GMC
- Mitsubishi
- Pontiac
- Mazda

### 3. Total Policies by Coverage Zone

Insurance policies are analyzed across different coverage zones:

- Urban
- Rural
- Highly Urban
- Suburban
- Highly Rural

### 4. Total Policies by Age Group

Policies are analyzed across customer age groups:

- 15–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 56–65
- 66–75

The dashboard allows comparison of policy distribution across different age segments.

### 5. Total Policies by Car Year

A time-series analysis is used to understand how the number of policies varies according to car year.

### 6. Total Policies by Education

The dashboard analyzes policies across education categories such as:

- High School
- Bachelors
- Masters
- PhD

### 7. Claim Amount by Education & Marital Status

A matrix is used to compare claim amounts across:

- Education
- Divorced
- Married
- Separated
- Single
